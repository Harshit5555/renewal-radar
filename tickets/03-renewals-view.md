# Ticket 03 (feature): Help my team stop missing renewals

**From:** Jordan Hayes, VP of Customer Success
**Priority:** Medium

My CSMs keep getting surprised by renewals. Today they scroll through the full
account list and do date math in their heads. Last month two renewals worth
about $90k lapsed because nobody noticed them coming until it was too late.

What I want is a renewals view my team opens on Monday morning and immediately
knows who to call:

1. Accounts renewing in the next 30, 60 or 90 days, soonest first.
2. Each CSM can see just their own accounts. I want to see everyone's.
3. At the top: how much ARR is up for renewal, and how much of that is at risk.
4. Make it obvious when a renewal is coming up and nobody is working on it.

Keep it simple. My team is not technical, and they will not read a manual.

Jordan
