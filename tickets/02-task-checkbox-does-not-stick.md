# Ticket 02: Checking off a task does not stick

**From:** Ana Ruiz, Customer Success Manager
**Priority:** High

When I tick a task as done on an account page, nothing happens. The box stays
unticked and the "open" count does not change. If I click it again, it finally
shows as done.

Yesterday I unticked a task I had marked by mistake. It looked unticked, but
after I refreshed the page it was back to done.

I do not trust the checklist anymore, so I have started tracking my tasks in a
notebook, and I know at least two others on the team are doing the same. That
defeats the point of the tool.

Ana
